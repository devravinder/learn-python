# Lesson 009 — Inferential Statistics & Hypothesis Testing

**Module:** 2 — Probability & Statistics
**Prerequisites:** [008](../008_descriptive-statistics/README.md)
**Estimated time:** 3 hours

## Objective

Go from "describing the data you have" (Lesson 008) to "drawing a justified
conclusion about data you don't have" — sampling, confidence intervals, and
hypothesis testing. This is the last lesson before Module 3's math track, and
the statistical reasoning behind A/B testing, model comparison, and "is this
result real or noise" questions you'll ask constantly once you're training
models.
