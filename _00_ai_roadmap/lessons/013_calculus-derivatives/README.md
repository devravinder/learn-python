**Module:** 3 — Linear Algebra & Calculus
**Prerequisites:** [002](../002_python-fundamentals-and-data-structures/README.md)
**Estimated time:** 2 hours

## Objective

The derivative measures how a function's output changes as its input
changes — the entire mathematical basis of "how should I adjust this weight
to reduce the loss." The chain rule is what makes backpropagation possible
through arbitrarily deep networks.
