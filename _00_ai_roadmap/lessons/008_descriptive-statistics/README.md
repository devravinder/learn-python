# Lesson 008 — Descriptive Statistics

**Module:** 2 — Probability & Statistics
**Prerequisites:** [007](../007_probability-distributions/README.md)
**Estimated time:** 2–3 hours

## Objective

Summarize a dataset with a handful of numbers (central tendency, spread,
shape, correlation) — the everyday first step of understanding any dataset
before modeling it, and the statistics behind metrics like variance and
covariance that reappear throughout ML.
