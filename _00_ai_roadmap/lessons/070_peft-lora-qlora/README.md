# Lesson 070 — Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning: LoRA & QLoRA

**Module:** 12 — Fine-Tuning & Alignment
**Prerequisites:** [012](../012_eigenvalues-eigenvectors-svd/README.md) (low-rank approximation), [069](../069_transfer-learning-fine-tuning/README.md)
**Estimated time:** 2.5 hours

## Objective

The technique that makes fine-tuning large models practical on consumer
hardware — freeze the pretrained weights entirely, learn a small low-rank
update instead. This is Lesson 012's low-rank approximation idea, put to
direct practical use.
