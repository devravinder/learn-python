## Objective

The exact training objective every LLM (including the one you'll build)
optimizes: predict the next token. Simple to state, and this lesson makes
sure the loss function, perplexity metric, and why this "obvious" task
produces genuinely intelligent-seeming behavior are all fully clear before
Lesson 063a starts training real models on it.
