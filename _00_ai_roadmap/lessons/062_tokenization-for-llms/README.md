# Lesson 062 — Tokenization for LLMs: BPE & SentencePiece

**Module:** 10 — Attention & Transformers (final lesson)
**Prerequisites:** [055](../055_text-preprocessing-tokenization/README.md)
**Estimated time:** 2 hours

## Objective

Lesson 055 introduced BPE conceptually with a character-level toy example.
This lesson covers what real LLM tokenizers actually do differently
(byte-level BPE, SentencePiece) and the practical tradeoffs in choosing a
vocabulary — direct preparation for Lesson 068a's from-scratch
implementation, following Karpathy's `minbpe`.
